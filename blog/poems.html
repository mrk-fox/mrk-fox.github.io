my poems

I like poems. Imagine that we'd communicate through music sounds. Notes are letters and words are chords. If we would speak, we'd play some chords that make sense but arent necessarily music. Poems are like music but with language and words.
I write poems in English, German and Russian.

The Year

Hey friend,

You know, this year is coming to an end.
And I think: was ist all worth it?
All the pain and all the loss that I had to cross
through this change of seasons and time,
epochs and rhymes?

I think yes.
Even through the sorrow and the pain,
the thin, golden rays of light do hit my heart
and melt the stain of darkness and inhumane things done to us.

It was a long, long way, but aren't we here to hope for the best,
and leave to the universe the rest?
So, thank you for this wonderful year.

You're the reason I write here,
you're the reason I hope for a better year,
better time,
better season,
epoch and rhyme.
You're the reason why I'm happy.

to somebody
